Self-paced online courses you can take through Wade's Tactical, 24/7. Carry license classroom training, a twelve-course foundation for new handgun owners, and the two courses nobody thinks about until they need them.

Read this before you buy anything

These are online courses, not licenses. Finishing one does not issue you a permit — it gets you the training and the certificate the state wants to see. The Texas LTC course covers the classroom portion only; you still have to qualify on the range, and that part you do with me here in Del Rio. Message me and I'll walk you through it.

The one I'd start with

Handgun Owner Foundations

Twelve courses, three to four hours, one purchase — the core knowledge every handgun owner should have, bundled into one path.

How handguns work. How to load, unload, store and carry them. How to pick a holster that keeps you safe and legal. And the legal side most people never get taught: buying and selling, transporting across state lines, where you cannot carry, and what to do when an officer walks up and you're armed.

If you're brand new, or you bought a gun during the panic and never got trained on it, this is where to start — then come see me on the range.
