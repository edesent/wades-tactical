Before you message

Three things worth knowing

01

You don't need a gun

Rentals are available and include ammunition. Come qualify on one, then buy the handgun that actually fits you afterward.

02

You don't need experience

Basics are included at no extra cost. First-time shooters are the students I've built the whole class around.

03

You do need to be 18

That's the state minimum for a Texas License to Carry — 18, not 21. Bring valid ID on class day.

Don't count on 7 minutes from 911.

Do your part as a law-abiding citizen. Get trained, get licensed, and carry with confidence.
