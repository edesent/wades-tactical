Never done this before

First-time shooters

If you have never fired a handgun in your life, you are not behind. You are exactly who this class was built for.

I have successfully instructed and taught thousands of new students how to carry with confidence. Nervous is normal. Nervous is fine. What we do about it is start at the beginning — grip, stance, sight picture, trigger press, safe handling — before anybody worries about a scorecard.

Basic training is included at no extra cost. Other instructors will make you pay $200 before you even take the LTC course. That has never sat right with me.

  • Grip, stance and how to actually hold the thing
  • Sight picture and trigger control, slowly, until it clicks
  • Loading, unloading, malfunctions and safe handling
  • Choosing a carry gun that fits your hands and your life
Don't let ugly guns, or feeling uncomfortable carrying a firearm, get in the way of you carrying every single day. — Wade's Tactical

No firearm yet?

Rentals available — ammunition included

Do not go buy a handgun just to take this class. Come qualify on a rental, put rounds through a few different guns, and figure out what actually fits your hand before you spend a dime at the counter.

Rental + ammo, one charge

The range qualification charge covers range use, your target, ammunition, and the rental firearm if you need one. Message me and I'll break down exactly what your situation costs.

Try before you buy

A Glock 43 is a single-stack 9mm and a genuinely practical everyday carry gun. Bigger hands? A 43X is about an inch longer in the grip and also a great choice. Shoot both.

Where to buy in Del Rio

The whole household

Family & youth range safety

Wade's Tactical is fully equipped to handle coachable children learning proper firearms safety. My own son started young, supervised, doing it right.

A gun in the house is a fact your kids already know about. Curiosity handled on a supervised range with hearing protection and a real set of rules is worth more than any lock you'll ever buy — and it goes hand in hand with proper storage, not instead of it.

  • Supervised, structured, one-on-one pace — no rushing a nervous kid
  • The four rules until they're automatic, not memorized
  • What to do if they ever find a firearm somewhere else
  • Spouses and partners welcome — a lot of couples take the LTC class together
