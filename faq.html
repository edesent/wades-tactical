Questions I get asked every week

If yours isn't here, send it. There is no such thing as a question that's too basic — the people who ask them are the ones who end up carrying safely.

The big one

Texas law

Doesn't Texas have constitutional carry? Why do I need a license?

Texas has permitless carry — not constitutional carry. They are not the same thing, and it is very easy to get a felony under the confusion of the two.

Permitless carry doesn't cover every person, every place, or every situation. A License to Carry gives you the best chance and the knowledge needed to stay lawful: where you can and cannot carry, what the law says about use of force, what your responsibilities are, and what happens after a defensive incident. It also opens up 37+ other states.

What are the actual benefits of holding an LTC?
  • Legal clarity — you know the boundaries instead of guessing at them
  • Reciprocity in 37 or more states besides Texas
  • Carry in some places permitless carry does not reach — the Prohibited Areas for Firearms course goes deeper on this
  • Exemption from the federal NICS check when you buy from a dealer, so purchases go faster
  • You've been formally trained on use of force, which matters enormously if you ever have to explain a decision
How old do I have to be?

Eighteen. You have to be at least 18 years of age or older to obtain a License to Carry from the state of Texas. A lot of people assume it's 21 — it isn't.

How many states will honor my Texas LTC?

37 or more states besides Texas, through reciprocity agreements. That list does shift as states change their laws, so check the current Texas DPS reciprocity map before a road trip — and remember that carrying legally in another state means following that state's rules while you're in it. If you drive or fly a lot, the Transporting a Firearm course is worth the hour.

Getting through it

The class itself

I've never fired a handgun. Am I going to embarrass myself?

No. First-time shooters are the whole reason I teach the way I teach. I have successfully instructed thousands of new students how to carry with confidence, and every single one of them started exactly where you are.

Basic instruction — grip, stance, sight picture, trigger, safe handling — is included at no extra cost. Other instructors make you pay $200 for a basics course before you're even allowed to take the LTC class.

What if I don't own a gun?

Rentals are available and they include ammunition. Honestly, this is the better way to do it if you're new — shoot a few different handguns during the class and buy the one that actually fits your hand instead of the one somebody at a counter talked you into.

How long does the class take?

Classes generally start at 9am on a weekend morning and run the classroom portion and the range qualification in the same day. Plan on the day, not on a couple of hours, and don't schedule anything tight right after.

What do I need to bring?

Valid ID, your handgun and holster if you have them, 50+ rounds of factory ammunition, eye and ear protection, closed-toe shoes, a hat, sunscreen and water. Renting? Then just ID, eyes and ears — the rest is covered. Full list is on the class page. Not sure about your holster? There's a whole Holster Selection course on it.

What's the shooting test actually like?

50 rounds at a B-27 silhouette — 20 at 3 yards, 20 at 7 yards, 10 at 15 yards. It's a marksmanship standard, not a tactical test, and people pass it who have never shot before that morning. We work through the basics first if you need them.

Can I take the classroom part online instead?

Yes. The online course is available 24/7 and covers the classroom portion. When you finish it, message me and we schedule your range day to qualify. Details here, or browse every online course I offer.

Money and logistics

Booking & cost

What does it cost?

Message me and I'll break the whole thing down for you — it depends on whether you're doing the in-person class or the online course, and whether you need a rental.

What I can tell you plainly: the range qualification is a separate charge from the course, and that charge covers range use, your target, ammunition, and the rental firearm if you need one. I've kept it fairly affordable on purpose. Local businesses have also sponsored discounts on classes before, so it's always worth asking what's running.

How do I reserve a seat?

Send a message and I'll put your name down. Seats are limited every class and they go fast — most are 50 to 75% reserved before I even announce them publicly. If a class is already full, ask to go on the cancellation list; spots do open.

Where are classes held?

Here in Del Rio. Students come in from Laughlin, Brackettville, Comstock and around Val Verde County. Message me for the exact location and start time for the class you're booking.

Everything else

Other training

Can my kids learn firearms safety here?

Yes. Wade's Tactical is fully equipped to handle coachable children learning proper firearms safety on the range — supervised, structured and at their pace. My own son started young, doing it the right way. If there are kids in the house, also take a look at the Firearm Security & Storage course.

Do you install red dots and work on guns?

I install pistol optics, sight them in, and teach you to shoot fast with one instead of hunting for the dot. I also do Cerakote refinishing and sight installation — tritium and fiber optic for day and night visibility. More here.

Do you offer anything outside Texas?

Yes — Wade's Tactical offers the California CCW course and renewal, plus the Florida CWL, for both residential and non-residential applicants. See all the license courses.
